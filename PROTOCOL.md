# Semantic feature family outage benchmark

## Contribution
Compare loss of a semantically defined calculation family with uniformly sampled random column outages of exactly the same cardinality. Family membership is specified by observable feature names and recorded as executable lists. These are simulated measurement-family failures, not validated failures of a particular deployed sensor process. No new classifier or MaskAug reproduction is claimed.

## Primary official-source experiment
RT-IoT2022, UCI API id 942. Numerical predictors; identifiers, ports and categorical predictors excluded. Identical (numeric vector, binary target) pairs are deduplicated. Conflicting binary labels are retained. Exact numeric-vector groups are partitioned before row subsampling, preventing the same input vector from crossing train/calibration/test. Twelve label-blind 60/20/20 group splits (seeds 1001–1012) use fixed caps 6000/1500/3000. Class-stratified subsampling occurs inside each group partition. The split indices and group memberships are saved.

12 configurations: RF median, repeated clean rows, independent masking, noise training; HistGB median, native NaN, native NaN plus masking; RF with reference-capped kNN imputation (256 training references, 5 neighbors); RF with iterative imputation (3 rounds, 5 nearest predictors); MLP clean, masking and noise training. Fixed settings, not architecture-level optimized rankings. Tree count 60, maximum RF depth 18. HistGB maximum iterations 80, 15 leaves, early stopping disabled. MLP widths 64/32, max iterations 100, internal early stopping. Augmented neural training is not update-budget matched to clean MLP.

Training-only median and standardization statistics. kNN and iterative imputers use training-standardized inputs. Native HistGB directly receives NaNs. Masked/noisy training adds one copy of original training rows. Training masks independently remove 30% of cells; noise training uses unbounded Gaussian noise at 0.1 training SD per feature. The clean repeated-row RF matches augmented row count/tree count, not time or optimizer updates.

27 evaluation conditions per fitted method: clean; four semantic outages; three count-matched random outages for each family; five noise ablations at alpha 0.1 and 0.3. Ablations: unbounded noise only, clipping only, clipping plus noise, clipping/rounding only, clipping/rounding plus noise. Clip-only and projection-only are deliberately repeated across alpha labels and are not independent observations. Zero-SD features receive no random noise. Bounds and integer detection use training rows only. Bounds do not enforce joint physical constraints.

All methods report fixed-threshold and calibration-threshold Macro-F1, recall, FPR and balanced accuracy; AUROC/AP; raw/calibrated Brier and 10-bin binary ECE. Platt scaling fits logit scores on clean held-out calibration rows. The decision threshold maximizes Youden J on those same calibration rows; neither calibration nor threshold uses test labels. This is standard held-out calibration and secondary evaluation, not a guarantee of transfer under corruption.

## Primary statistical family
For each method and semantic family, difference = fixed-threshold Macro-F1 under semantic loss minus the mean of its three count-matched random losses in the same split. All 48 comparisons are jointly adjusted by Holm. Corrected resampled t approximation: SE = sqrt((1/12 + n_test/n_train) s_d^2), with n_test/n_train=0.5 and 11 degrees of freedom. Two-sided p-values. Pointwise 95% intervals use this corrected SE; they are not simultaneous family intervals. The approximation addresses overlapping resampling heuristically and does not turn rows into independent network environments. No capture/site-level significance claim is made. Other contrasts are descriptive secondary analyses.

## Temporal corrected CICIDS2017 case
Complete official author-hosted LYCOS Tuesday ZIP is downloaded and CRC checked. SHA-256 is recorded. Original author's Tuesday labeling predicates are translated literally; no labels are inferred from row order. Three windows: training ends at 12:30, 12:40 or 12:50 UTC on 2017-07-04; the next 10 minutes are calibration; the subsequent 15 minutes are test. Flow end = start timestamp + flow_duration. Training/calibration flows crossing boundaries are excluded, as are test flows ending beyond their window. Up to 6000/1500/3000 rows are sampled within chronological partitions. Twelve seeds per window and four clean-trained baselines yield 144 fits and 1872 metric rows. The windows overlap and share one capture day; seed dispersion is descriptive, not an independent temporal CI. The selected windows cover benign/FTP brute-force traffic; they do not cover every LYCOS attack class or capture day. LYCOS is not the Engelen/Liu corrected release.

## Expanded exploratory controls version 0.4.0
Five representative configurations (RF median, RF mask, Hist median, Hist native, MLP clean) are refitted on each of the twelve original RT partitions. Their semantic Macro-F1 values must reproduce the original values exactly. For each family, a training-only pool of 20,000 cardinality-matched random column sets supplies twenty uniform subsets, twenty approximate marginal-information matches and twenty approximate joint information/correlation matches. Exact semantic-set duplicates are excluded; partial overlap is allowed. Group descriptors are summed univariate training-label mutual information after decile discretization, mean absolute within-group Spearman correlation, and constant-column fraction. Matching minimizes squared standardized descriptor discrepancies; candidate-pool SD supplies the scales. Matching is approximate and does not control conditional information or all dependence. Diagnostics accompany every selected subset.

The same design is replicated on the complete official LYCOS Thursday archive. The original authors exclude afternoon traffic; their morning predicates identify web brute-force, XSS and SQL injection. Training ends before 12:45 UTC; calibration starts at 12:45 and ends before 13:20; testing starts at 13:20 and ends before 14:00 on 2017-07-06. Flow ends must remain within their partition. Twelve stratified samples use 6000/1500/3000 caps. Feature-space duplicate independence is not enforced in this chronological case; temporal order does not imply host/session independence.

The expanded tests are explicitly exploratory, selected after the original analysis. They do not establish semantic causality. All 120 expanded method/family/comparator contrasts across both datasets receive one pooled Holm correction; separate 60-test values are also retained for audit but are not the primary expanded significance report. Corrected pointwise intervals use the same dependence approximation and n_test/n_train=0.5. Original 48-test results are preserved and identified separately. New control evaluations use fixed raw threshold 0.5, AUROC/AP and Brier; they do not refit corruption-specific calibration.

Version 0.4.0 removes unused XGBoost, LightGBM and unused-test references from the manuscript, normalizes bibliography fields, uses grouped bars for the unordered noise interventions, and reports model-dependent effect directions. Source access and publication status remain separate from scientific claims. A request for MaskAug full text and reproduction materials was sent to its publisher-identified corresponding author from the confirmed university email on 2026-11-13; receipt is not assumed.

## Secondary ranking contrasts
The same semantic/comparator draws and held-out records yield paired AUROC and non-interpolated AP contrasts, after averaging comparator draws within each seed. All 48 original and 120 expanded contrasts are retained. The same approximate corrected pointwise intervals (12 seeds, test/train ratio 0.5, 11 df) are descriptive, not simultaneous, and no new p-values or adjusted findings are added. AP is prevalence dependent; cross-dataset raw AP is not an effect estimate. Opposite point-estimate signs alone do not prove significant metric disagreement or equivalence of clean/corrupted ranking. No further fits or data collection are required.
